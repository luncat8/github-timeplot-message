# 0.5.0 - plan

## Goal

Five draft items around the demo/screenshot workflow and two quality fixes; the
"users who used this tool" wall is deferred to a later version.

1. **Demo button** — simulate a fetch + painted year in one click: light
   typical-account background noise and a centered smooth `hello world`, with
   the typical recommended mapping (low 5, high 10). For screenshots and quick
   looks without a github account.
2. **README header screenshot** — rendered in the sandbox with playwright
   (`experiments/header-shot.js`) → `header.png`, embedded at the top of the
   README.
3. **Today centered** — fresh sessions start at offset `+26`, which puts
   today's column (always `52 - offset`) on the center column of the 53-week
   grid: half a year of history on the left, half a year of plan on the right.
   Saved sessions keep their stored offset.
4. **Track follows the plot** — the week-offset track is inverted: dragging the
   thumb right now shifts the plot's dates (and the today cell) right, the same
   direction the content moves when scrolling the plot. End labels swap
   (`+52w` left, `-52w` right); arrow keys become spatial (right = thumb right =
   older). The today marker stays in the center of the track.
5. **Better recommendation after fetch** — replace the 90-day mean with robust
   quantiles over the same 90 days (today excluded): `low = median day`,
   `high = p90 busy day`, floored at `2*low` / `low + MIN_SPREAD`, capped at
   `MAP_MAX`. Spikes and inactive stretches no longer drag the numbers;
   status reports `med / p90`.
6. **Better smooth font** — fit and center the text on the measured glyph
   bounding box (`actualBoundingBoxAscent/Descent`) instead of the em-box
   middle with a hard-coded 0.72 cap-height guess; exact vertical centering for
   caps, lowercase and descenders, slightly larger fill (0.96 w / 0.94 h).

## Notes

- Demo noise: seeded LCG (reproducible screenshots), weekday-weighted, slow
  busy-season waves, ~10% of cells at level 1, rare level 2. Text merges over
  the noise (`smoothText(text, replace)` gains an explicit `replace` argument;
  the text row passes the checkbox, the demo passes `false`).
- The demo writes its state into the visible controls (text input `hello
  world`, font `smooth`) so what happened on screen is reproducible by render.
- Track geometry helpers stay symmetric: `offsetToX` mirrors, `xToOffset`
  mirrors back; the filled green span between the today marker and the thumb,
  ticks and hover guide are unchanged.
- `recommendMapping(map, todayMs, out)` collects the 90 daily counts into a
  module-level scratch array, sorts in place (insertion sort), and fills
  `out.low/high/med/p90`. `meanDailyCommits` is gone (no remaining callers).
