# 0.6.0 worklog — unify slider center with today-centered view

## What was built

Follow-up to 0.5.0's centering: 0.5.0 centered today's *column* in the plot at
`offset = 26`, but the slider's today-marker (and thumb at rest) sat at `offset 0`
— and at `offset 0` today fell on the **right edge** of the grid. So the slider
center and the today-in-plot were not centered together (draft.txt TODO line 56).

Fix: redefine the offset origin so `offset 0` is the centered view. `gridStartMs`
now starts `26` weeks (= `(W-1)/2`) before this week's Sunday, plus the offset;
today's column is `26 - offset`, so `offset 0` → column 26 (the center of 53).
That makes three things coincide at the slider's middle: the today marker, the
rest-thumb, and today's cell. Direction is unchanged (thumb-left = future,
thumb-right = past; wheel down = into the past; Home/End/Arrows spatial).

The centered grid window at `offset 0` is byte-identical to 0.5.0's at `offset 26`
(same `gridStartMs` result), so existing centered views are visually preserved —
only the offset label and thumb position change (thumb moves from 1/4-left to
center).
